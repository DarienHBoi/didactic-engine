A complete overhaul of the Forever chat, in the game's own style. It's built on Blizzard's own chat window, so your channels, colors, ignore list and filter addons keep working.

## Look

![Everchat in the gold theme and in the minimal style](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2079/870/portada-png.png)

- A header for your tabs, the open one framed in gold, and a side bar for your buttons
- Gold, silver and bronze themes, or a flat minimal style
- Fades at rest like the game's chat, hides until you need it, or stays always visible
- Five fonts with every symbol players type, or any font from your SharedMedia packs

## Tabs

- Tabs with new messages get a dot in the messages' color
- Each tab can have its own sound, from war drums to a murloc
- Drag tabs to sort them and add new ones with the +
- Copy your tabs to your other characters

## Messages

- Your guild's crest in place of [Guild]
- Party and raid icons, with a crown on the leader
- Icons for items, spells and quests
- Race and class portraits next to names
- Your name in gold when someone mentions you

## Less noise

- [1. General - Stonetalon Mountains] becomes [1. General]
- Give any channel a name and color of your own
- Surnames in full, as an initial or hidden
- Repeated ads show once every five minutes
- Lines in Chinese, Korean or Russian can be hidden

## Tools

- Your chat comes back after a reload
- Search the chat, copy all of it or a single line
- Click "inv" in a whisper to invite
- Resize from the corner, move by the header, lock with a Shift-click

The options open from the gear on the side bar or with /everchat.

---

Everchat speaks English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian and Russian. Saved chat stays on your computer. Found a bug or have an idea? Tell me [on GitHub](https://github.com/Lokzsxf/everchat-issues/issues) or in the comments.
